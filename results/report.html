Verdict

  • Good at: layout, speaker names, spelling, old-English style. It even pairs ROMEO with JULIET and LADY CAPULET.
  • Weak at: meaning. Sentences sound like Shakespeare but don't make sense.
  • Why: it reads single characters and learned from only 1 MB of text, so most of its effort goes on spelling.
  • Best settings for writing: temperature about 0.8, top-k 10.

6. Setup

  • Data: Tiny Shakespeare (1.1M characters), split by position into 90% train / 5% val / 5% test.
  • Tokenizer: 65 characters, built from the train text only.
  • Model: 6 layers, 6 heads, width 384, context 256 characters, dropout 0.2, GPT-2 design.
  • Training: 5000 steps, batch 64, AdamW, learning rate 0.001 with warm-up then cosine to 0.0001, weight decay 0.1, gradient clip 1.0.
  • Next ideas: a BPE tokenizer (word pieces), more data (TinyStories), stopping earlier.
